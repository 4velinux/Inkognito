    What Inkognito does with your text

    • Nothing leaves this page. A content security policy blocks every outbound connection, so even a bug could not send your text anywhere.
    • No fonts, scripts, analytics or images load from other servers. The counter on the left shows live proof.
    • Drafts and settings are saved in this browser's local storage for this address only. Erase them any time.
    • The voice check also scans for emails, phone numbers, card numbers, ID numbers, API keys, internal addresses and tracking links before you post. The scan runs on your device.

    Check it yourself: open your browser's developer tools, Network tab, and use the app.
